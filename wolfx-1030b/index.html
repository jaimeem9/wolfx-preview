Who is WolfX Signals?
A trading community that shares its market analysis on Telegram. This page is about copy trading: the trades of our account, the one audited live by Myfxbook, are copied into an account in your name. We are not a broker and we do not hold your money.
Can I lose money?
Yes. Trading carries a high risk of loss, and the record above includes losing months. The higher the risk level you choose, the bigger both the gains and the losses. Only use money you can afford to lose.
Who holds my money?
You do. The account is opened in your name with the broker, the same way you would open one at a bank. We have no access to your funds and we cannot withdraw from your account. All we do is send the trades.
Can I take my money out whenever I want?
Yes. It is your account, so deposits and withdrawals are between you and the broker. There is no lock up on our side.
What happens if I want to stop?
You switch the copying off and no new trades open in your account. Your account, your balance and any open positions stay yours.
Do I need to know anything about trading?
No. You choose your risk level and the trades are copied for you. If you want to understand what is traded and why, the analysis is shared on Telegram.
How do I start?
Tap Start Here and message WolfX Support on Telegram. The team walks you through connecting your account and answers any question before you connect anything.
