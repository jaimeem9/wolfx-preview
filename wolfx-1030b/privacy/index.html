Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This page explains what information WolfX Signals collects when you visit this website and how it is used. By using this website you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

This policy applies only to information collected through this website. It does not apply to information collected offline or through channels we do not own or control.

Information we collect

This website does not ask you to fill in any form. If you contact us, for example through Telegram, we may receive your name, username, phone number, the contents of your message and any other information you choose to share.

We also collect browsing data through advertising tools such as the Meta Pixel, for example the pages you visit, the time spent on each page and the source that brought you here.

How we use your information

  • Provide, operate and maintain this website.
  • Understand and analyze how visitors use it, and improve it.
  • Answer your messages and help you get started.
  • Measure the performance of our advertising.
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.

Log files

Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps log files. They record your IP address, browser type, internet service provider, date and time, and referring and exit pages. They are used to administer the site and analyze trends, and are not linked to information that identifies you personally.

Cookies, Meta Pixel and Conversions API

This website uses cookies and the Meta Pixel. When you visit a page or tap a button to message us, the Meta Pixel and the Meta Conversions API send Meta information such as your IP address, browser details, the page visited and the action taken. Meta uses it to measure the results of our ads and to show relevant ads. Meta handles that information under its own Privacy Policy.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage the ads you see in your Facebook or Instagram ad preferences.

Third parties

We share personal data with service providers only as far as needed to run this website and our services, such as hosting, advertising and messaging providers. They may not use it for any other purpose.

Your rights under the GDPR

  • Access: ask for a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • Restriction: ask us to restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • Objection: object to the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • Portability: ask us to transfer your data to another organization or to you, under certain conditions.

Your rights under the CCPA

California residents may ask us to disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data we have collected, to delete it, and not to sell it.

Contact

To exercise any of these rights or ask about this policy, message WolfX Support on Telegram. We reply to requests within one month.
