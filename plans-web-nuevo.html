FAQs

Once I make the payment, how can I join the VIP group?

Once the payment is done, you will receive an email confirming your subscription with your Telegram Access Link.

In case you have any problem after completing the payment, contact our Telegram live chat support, where a real human agent will assist you.

What is the Copytrading Service and how does it work?

Our private trading bot manages your entire account automatically. No experience or time needed.

It is completely different from our VIP Signals and Auto Trading Tools. With VIP Signals you receive trading signals and follow them yourself, manually or automatically with tools like Cornix or TSC. With the Copytrading Service, our private bot trades your account for you: you never execute trades, manage positions or follow signals.

How it works: you open and fund your own trading account. It always stays under your name and your control. We then connect our private bot, which executes every trade automatically.

  • No trading experience required
  • No need to monitor charts or execute signals
  • Your funds stay in your own broker account
  • Verified results available
  • More stable and lower risk than signals

The only requirement is being an active VIP member. Included free with every Lifetime plan.

Is it 100% automated?

Yes, with our Copytrading Service. Once your account is connected, our private bot manages everything on its own, following our strategy with our risk management. You never need to execute trades, follow signals or watch charts. No experience and no time required.

If you prefer to stay in control, you can also follow our VIP Signals manually or automatically with tools like Cornix. The choice is yours.

How does the Funded Accounts Service work?

We help you get funded by passing the prop firm exams for you, so members with less capital can trade bigger and boost their profits. Ask our support team and we’ll walk you through the whole process.

Do I need experience to follow the signals?

No. Every signal comes with entry, stop loss and 3 take profits, so you know exactly what to do. Beginners follow them step by step, and our Trading Academy (included in Lifetime) teaches you the rest from the ground up.

How many signals do you send and when?

Forex + Gold and Crypto each get 8 to 15 high-quality signals per week. The All Markets plan combines both, so you get 16 to 30 signals per week with full diversification across forex, gold and crypto.

It’s the plan we recommend to make the most of every market. All signals are sent live on Telegram, when market conditions are best, with most activity between 10am and 10pm (UTC+1).

Monthly or Lifetime, and can I upgrade later?

Monthly is a subscription you can cancel any time. Lifetime is a one-off payment with no renewals that also unlocks the Copytrading Service and the Trading Academy at no extra cost.

You can upgrade or switch from monthly to lifetime whenever you want. Just contact our support team.
